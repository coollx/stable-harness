`docs/framing.md` is the backbone of the paper. Write it as a paper section: declarative sentences, every quantity defined once with a symbol and a citation, no hedging, no meta-commentary such as "candidate contribution" or "this will be revisited"; uncertainty appears only as an (open) marker. In discussion, state what is documented (with file:line) separately from what is inferred, and describe a component by its input, output and where it is used rather than by adjectives such as "cheap" or "stand-in".

**Why:** On 2026-09-28 the researcher rejected "a cheap stand-in for the protocol; the estimator never enters the reported numbers" as vague and defensive, and asked that framing text read as a top-tier ML paper; earlier the same day they asked which claims were documented and which inferred.

**How to apply:** Before editing framing.md, reread the sentence as a reviewer would; delete hedges and self-reference. In explanations, label sources and inferences. See also [[explain-context-before-term]].
