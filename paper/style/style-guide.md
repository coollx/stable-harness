# Style Guide

Section 1 is the fixed core: craft rules that hold for any paper, venue, or field.
Every later section is paper-specific, appended only by an approved `/paper:style-synthesis` proposal; the guide is never edited without the researcher's explicit approval.
Check this guide before drafting or revising any section; later sections refine earlier ones where they are more specific.

## 1. Core (fixed)

**Narration.**
- The paper tells one story: every section discharges a named link in the argument chain, and a reader skimming only first sentences can reconstruct the whole argument.
- Topic-sentence-first, always: every paragraph opens with the sentence that states its single job; one idea per paragraph; internal order claim → support → consequence, ending on the payoff, never a trailing detail.
- One adversative turn per pivot: flip background into problem with a single connective, then answer the crystallized gap in the next paragraph's first sentence.
- State each fact exactly once, then refer back by its name or a demonstrative noun phrase ("This limitation"); never re-explain.
- Purpose before mechanism in every sentence introducing a design or analysis: open with the goal it serves, never a bare structural description.
- Support points backward: never cite material the reader has not reached; restate the mechanism in one clause instead.

**Claims and evidence.**
- Anchor every result claim at its point of assertion to a named table, figure, or section.
- State each result one way: when a result is restated across abstract, introduction, results, conclusion, or a caption, the number and its bounding word match verbatim ("up to 4x" never drifts to "nearly 4x", "surpasses" never softens to "matches"); check every occurrence before submission.
- Use the strongest quantifier the results fully support: "all/every/consistently" only when no counterexample exists; otherwise scope the claim where it is made.
- Every claim the abstract or introduction makes is proven at one findable place in the paper, reusing the claim's key word so the reader can trace promise to proof.
- Make headline numbers recomputable at their anchor: restate both operands or tie the delta to a named condition.
- A relative number names its referent and denominator at first mention.

**Register.**
- Active voice, strong specific verbs for your own work; neutral-to-critical verbs for prior work; no hype adjectives, no unquantified superlatives.
- Calibrated hedging is asymmetric: assert your measured results flatly; hedge generality, scope, and speculation.
- Discharge every value word with a mechanism clause ("more productively by ...").
- Define every term at first use; coin no acronyms beyond the paper's named artifacts; no metaphors or aphorisms in paper prose.
- Emphasis is scarce: italics at a construct's first definition only; never emphasize clauses or sentences.
- No defensive writing: never deny a failure mode, safeguard, or misreading the reader has no reason to suspect; state each mechanism once, positively, where it belongs; pre-empt a genuine objection with evidence, never a bare denial.
- Never leave a negative result bare: pair the conceded number with a localized reason or defensible reframe and recover on what the numbers support; if no defensible explanation exists, state it plainly and do not invent one.
- The press-conference-principle skill decides what is said at all; this guide decides how every surviving sentence is written.

**Process.**
- Extract the template paper's section verbatim before drafting, never a summary; derive the target's sentence budget, opening forms, and number density; draft against that, not against your previous draft.
- Mismatch analysis before any revision: compare the draft with the template's corresponding section, report the misalignments, get approval, then rewrite.
- Draft in chat, write to files only on approval; re-read already-drafted sections before writing a new one so no fact is re-stated at the same altitude.
- Floats read standalone: captions decode every non-obvious mark, band, and reference line; results-figure captions end on a takeaway; every float is visually read in the rendered PDF before being called done.
